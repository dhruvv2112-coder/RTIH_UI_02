One local server, three live-linked apps

Pilgrim SOS reaches the Command Centre in under a second, Command dispatches staff, and the field app's accept, notes and completion flow straight back. Open each app in its own window — or on phones on the same Wi-Fi.

Try the end-to-end flow (2 minutes)

  1. Open all three apps side by side (or the Staff and Pilgrim apps on two phones via the QR codes).
  2. Pilgrim app → press and hold the red SOS bar for 1 second.
  3. Command → a critical “Pilgrim SOS” alert appears at the top of the queue with a chime. Press Dispatch, pick Ramesh K (marked “Field app · online”) and send.
  4. Staff app → the full-screen Incoming Dispatch appears. Hold Accept for 1 second.
  5. Pilgrim app now shows “Help is on the way — Ramesh Kumar”. Command shows “accepted · en route”.
  6. Staff app → add a note, then hold Complete Task. The alert resolves on Command and the pilgrim sees “Help reached you”.
  7. Also try: staff Field Report → new alert on Command; Command Emergency protocol (top bar) → banner on both phones.
